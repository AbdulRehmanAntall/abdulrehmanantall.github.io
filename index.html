About

I like building systems that have to deal with something messy, ambiguous and constantly changing. Then working out why they succeed when they do, and why they fail confidently when they don’t.

Large language models make that especially interesting. Every new architecture, tokenizer, training objective or efficiency trick changes what a model can learn, and what it struggles to understand.

I’m most interested in what happens when we take these systems beyond the languages they were mostly built around. There are thousands of languages, radically different ways of expressing the same ideas, and nowhere near enough data or compute to treat each one like English. That gap makes NLP an exciting systems problem: build better models, make them more efficient, and understand what they actually learned along the way.
